Aphrodite

Aphrodite (Greek: Ἀφροδίτη, Aphroditē) was one of the Twelve Olympians in ancient Greek religion and myth, goddess of love, beauty, pleasure, and procreation. In Homeric epic, Aphrodite is the daughter of Zeus and Dione.

Apollo

Apollo (Greek: Ἀπόλλων, Apollōn) was one of the Twelve Olympians in ancient Greek religion and myth, god of music, poetry, art, oracles, archery, plague, medicine, sun, light and knowledge. In Homeric epic, Apollo is the son of Zeus and Leto.

Medusa

Medusa (Greek: Μέδουσα, Medousa) was one of the three Gorgon sisters in ancient Greek religion and myth, known for her snake-like hair and the ability to turn people to stone with a glance. In Homeric epic, Medusa is the daughter of Phorcys and Ceto.

Poseidon

Poseidon (Greek: Ποσειδῶν, Poseidōn) was one of the Twelve Olympians in ancient Greek religion and myth, god of the sea, storms, earthquakes and horses. In Homeric epic, Poseidon is the son of Cronus and Rhea, brother of Zeus and Hades, and king of Atlantis.
